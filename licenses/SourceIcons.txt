TokenLens data-source icons
==========================

Bundled locally; the application does not contact image CDNs at runtime.
Brand names and marks belong to their respective owners. These assets identify
data sources; their inclusion does not imply endorsement.

Official assets
---------------
- Gemini CLI: https://geminicli.com/brand-kit/ and
  https://geminicli.com/brandassets/gemini-cli-icon_full-color.svg
- Pi: https://pi.dev/logo-auto.svg (linked by the official repository README).
- Aider: https://aider.chat/assets/icons/android-chrome-384x384.png
  (maintainer confirmation: https://github.com/Aider-AI/aider/issues/1100).
- Continue: https://docs.continue.dev/favicon.ico (official site wordmark;
  cropped to its symbol and resized to 64 pixels).
- Chatbox: https://chatboxai.app/favicon.ico (official app icon, resized).

Brand artwork collections
-------------------------
Claude, Codex, DeepSeek, OpenCode, Zhipu, Kimi, Cline, GitHub Copilot, LM Studio,
Ollama, Cursor, Trae, Windsurf, Cherry Studio, CodeBuddy, Kiro, Qoder and Goose:
@lobehub/icons-static-svg 1.95.1, https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons (MIT).
These preserve brand artwork; they are third-party reproductions rather than
an assertion that vendors supplied or endorsed the files. Monochrome assets
have a fixed dark fill for readability on the source icon's white tile.

JetBrains, Zed and Warp: Simple Icons https://simpleicons.org (CC0-1.0).
Exact upstream slugs: jetbrains, zedindustries, warp.
Simple Icons records upstream brand guidelines and trademark provenance in
https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/_data/simple-icons.json

Unverified source-specific logos retain a text monogram rather than substituting
another product's mark. import.svg is original TokenLens artwork under the
project's license.
